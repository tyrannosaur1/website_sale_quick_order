Quick Order by Reference
A business customer who reorders the same twenty articles every month should not have to find twenty product pages. Paste the list, keep the references you already use, and let the shop fill the cart.
website_sale onlySigned-in customersOPL-1
One line, one article
The reference and the quantity, separated by a space, a tab, a semicolon or a comma. Whatever the spreadsheet column already looks like, it pastes.
A typo costs one line
A reference that cannot be read is listed with its line number; the lines around it still reach the cart. Nobody retypes an order because of one character.
The shop decides, not the page
Prices, publication, pricelist and stock are the shop's own rules. The page adds lines through the same door the product page uses, so the two cannot disagree.
How a list is written
One product per line. The quantity is the last number on the line and everything before it is the reference, so a reference that contains spaces works as it is written on the invoice.
What is typedWIDGET-01 2

What it meansFour lines, four articles. Blank lines are ignored and do not shift the numbering the answer quotes back.
The same reference twiceSummed, not duplicated: two lines of WIDGET-01 become one line of the cart with the total. The merge ignores case.
Which referenceThe product's internal reference first, its barcode second. Two products sharing a reference is an ambiguity, and an ambiguity is reported rather than guessed.
The capA hundred lines per submission. Past that the list is refused whole, before anything is looked up.
What the customer sees
✓A menu entry in the shop header, next to the cart, for every signed-in visitor
✓A visitor who is not signed in is sent to the login page and comes back to the same page afterwards
✓A summary that says how many lines were added, and what each refused line said
✓The text the customer typed stays in the textarea, so a corrected line is one edit away
✓A link back to the cart, and the cart's own quantity badge kept up to date
The edges, stated plainly
Not publishedA product hidden from the website is a line error, not a silent add. Archived and not-for-sale products are refused the same way, with their own sentence.
PricelistsA pricelist that names products -- a product, a variant or a category rule -- sells those products; a global rule adds the rest of the catalogue. A pricelist that names nothing is a general one and sells everything.
StockWhatever the shop does when a product is out of stock, this page does too: it adds through the shop's own path and turns a refusal into a line error instead of forcing the line in.
QuantitiesPositive numbers only. Zero, a negative number, a word or a line with no quantity at all is reported on its own line.
The formPosts back to the shop with its CSRF token, like every other form of the site. There is no JSON endpoint to call without one.
